About Orcado Fish

Orcado Fish combines satellite-driven analytics with applied ocean science to provide real-time insights that improve scouting accuracy, reduce operational risk, and enable sustainable harvest decisions.

Our mission is to empower fleets, researchers, and fisheries networks with actionable maritime intelligence — where science, data and sustainability work together.

How We Work

Precision PFZ scouting powered by satellite analytics
Real-time SST, chlorophyll & current modelling
Sustainable harvest planning with N8FISH systems

Why Our Data Matters

High-resolution environmental intelligence helps fleets operate efficiently, cut fuel costs, and align harvesting with sustainability standards.

Faster identification of productive marine zones
Optimized routes and reduced fuel expenditure
Responsible stock protection & resource balance
Catch-to-dock traceability & reporting integrity

Every harvest is mapped with verified GPS origin and handling records.
Sourced along nutrient-rich thermal fronts where ocean ecosystems thrive.
